RUN OF SHOW: SACRIFICIAL CONVERSATIONS × AJ HARDCASTLE (THE BLISSFULL EPISODE)
Private for Teddy & Monica — Host talking points only

Episode Theme: The Blueprint to Bliss: Growth, Grace, and Glowing Up with AJ Hardcastle
Primary Goal: Drive traffic and sales to myblissfull.com through organic host endorsement and founder storytelling.
Total Runtime: ~45 Minutes

────────────────────────────────────────
[0:00 - 5:00] INTRO & MONICA'S "CONFESSION" (THE HOOK)
────────────────────────────────────────

Vibe: High energy, welcoming, highly complimentary.

Teddy Script Idea:
(Teddy) "Welcome back to Sacrificial Conversations. Today we are talking about Growth, Grace, and... glowing skin? I have to be honest, Monica has been holding out on me, and our studio smells incredible right now."

Monica's Endorsement:
(Monica) "Listen, I have a confession. I am completely obsessed. I have been using the Blissfull Body Soufflé and the Hair Serum, and it has completely changed my routine. I had to get the creator in the studio. Welcome the founder of Blissfull, AJ Hardcastle!"

Icebreaker (either host can lead; AJ answers):
"When someone tells you your product changed their daily routine, does that feeling ever get old?"

────────────────────────────────────────
[5:00 - 15:00] SEGMENT 1: THE PRODUCT DEEP-DIVE (SHOW, TELL, & SELL)
────────────────────────────────────────

Goal: Pitch the products immediately while the audience is hooked by Monica's endorsement. Have the Soufflé and Serum on the table.

Monica's Angle (The User):
(Monica) "AJ, for the WFTB radio listeners who can't see this jar, walk us through the Body Soufflé. I know it has Mango butter, Jojoba, and Shea. Why these specific ingredients? What makes this different from the stuff we buy at the big box stores?"

Teddy's Angle (The Curious Co-Host):
(Teddy) "Now, AJ, I'm a guy. I see 'Hair Serum' and 'Body Soufflé'. Is this an exclusive club, or can men get in on this Blissfull lifestyle? How are guys using your products?"
(Producer Note: This expands her customer base live on air.)

────────────────────────────────────────
[15:00 - 25:00] SEGMENT 2: THE "SACRIFICE" OF THE START-UP (THE BUSINESS GRIND)
────────────────────────────────────────

Goal: Transition into the podcast's core theme of Growth, focusing on the reality of female entrepreneurship.

Talking Points & Questions:

The Kitchen Phase:
(Teddy) "Every great product starts as a messy experiment. Where were you in your life when you mixed your very first batch of what would become Blissfull?"

The Leap of Faith:
(Monica) "At what point did you look at this jar and say, 'This isn't just for me and my friends anymore. This is a real business.' Talk to us about the moment you had to bet on yourself."

Scaling Up:
(Teddy) "Scaling a physical product company is incredibly hard. What has been the biggest growing pain in taking Blissfull from an idea to a full-fledged e-commerce brand at myblissfull.com?"

────────────────────────────────────────
[25:00 - 35:00] SEGMENT 3: NOURISHING THE SOUL (FAITH & BALANCE)
────────────────────────────────────────

Goal: Ground the conversation in the hosts' values without losing the lifestyle appeal.

Talking Points & Questions:

The Balance:
(Monica) "Your brand's tagline is 'Nourish & Hydrate.' As a founder, how do you make sure you are nourishing your own soul and family when the business is demanding 110% of your energy?"

Overcoming 'No':
(Teddy) "Every business owner hits a wall. Can you share a moment where your faith was tested in this business, and how you pushed through it?"

────────────────────────────────────────
[35:00 - 45:00] SEGMENT 4: THE VISION & THE "SACRIFICIAL" WRAP-UP
────────────────────────────────────────

Goal: A powerful closing that drives the audience to purchase.

The Future:
(Monica) "You have the hair serum, the body soufflé... what is the ultimate vision? Where is the Blissfull brand 5 years from now?"

The Signature Question:
(Teddy) "AJ, to live a truly 'Blissfull' life and build a successful brand, what is one thing you've had to lovingly sacrifice along the way?"

The Hard Outro:
(Teddy) Lead the handoff: "Monica, tell them one more time what they need to buy. AJ, tell them exactly where to go."
(Monica) Spell out what you use and love (Body Soufflé, Hair Serum) one more time for radio listeners.
(Producer Note: Ensure myblissfull.com is spelled out clearly for audio listeners — letter by letter if needed.)
