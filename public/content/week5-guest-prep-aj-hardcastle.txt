III. CONVERSATION ROADMAP (GENERAL TOPICS)
────────────────────────────────────────

1. Monica’s endorsement & product deep-dive
   Right away, Monica will share how much she loves your line. We’ll ask you to walk through the formulation — why Mango Butter, Shea, Jojoba — and what makes Blissfull different from typical big-box options for people who only hear us on radio. Teddy may ask whether men can use the products too; a short pitch for male customers is great.

2. The leap of faith (building the brand)
   The messy reality of growing an e-commerce brand: your “aha” moment when Blissfull became a real business, the hustle, scaling pains, and the faith it took to bet on yourself.

3. Balancing the grind with “bliss”
   Your brand is about nourish and hydrate. As the founder, how do you protect your own soul, mental space, and family when the business wants 110%?

4. The signature “sacrificial” question
   To match our show title: to get to the success and peace you have now, what is one thing you’ve had to sacrifice along the way?
